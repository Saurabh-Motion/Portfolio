ABOUT SAURABH

I'm Saurabh Kumar, a motion graphics designer and visual architect who brings static vectors to life through rhythm, form, and fluid motion.

I think with curiosity, design with intention, and move ideas until they feel right in the gut, not just the grid.

HOW I WORK

How I approach projects is simple: I start by understanding the "why" behind the idea. Whether I'm crafting high-impact performance ads, intricate vector animations, or full-scale digital explainers, I look for the rhythmic thread that ties typography, timing, and visual pacing together.

As I continue to evolve professionally, I remain committed to refining my craft, staying curious, and letting each project teach me something new. The tools might change, the mediums might shift, but the intent stays the same: to create work that feels thoughtful, honest, and quietly powerful.
